# Capstone case: Northstar Outfitters

Northstar is a fictional outdoor retailer with e-commerce and 180 stores in four autonomous regions. Customers see stock online but must call stores to reserve it. Nightly inventory feeds are often stale, store processes vary, and 12% of collection orders are cancelled. Northstar wants click-and-collect in 60 pilot stores within nine months, followed by scale-out.

## Drivers and constraints

- Grow omnichannel revenue and customer trust.
- Reduce cancellation below 4% and confirm reservations within two minutes.
- Respect regional operating autonomy while standardizing critical semantics/interfaces.
- Reuse viable store systems; capital and specialist capacity are constrained.
- Protect customer data and maintain service during seasonal peaks.

## Cast

| Stakeholder | Authority/interest | Initial concern |
|---|---|---|
| Board sponsor | funding and strategic outcomes | measurable value within nine months |
| COO | operating model | consistent fulfillment at sustainable cost |
| Regional directors | local performance | loss of autonomy and migration disruption |
| Store managers | day-to-day adoption | workload and exception handling |
| Chief Information Security Officer | security assurance | identity, privacy, fraud, audit |
| Product/delivery leads | implementation | stable guardrails and fast decisions |
