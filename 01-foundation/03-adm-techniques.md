# F3 — Introduction to ADM techniques

## Outcomes

Match core techniques to their purpose and recognize when several techniques work together.

> **See it in practice:** Watch techniques connect [from an uncertain symptom to a safe migration](../examples/foundation/03-technique-chain.md).

| Technique | Question answered | Lightweight output |
|---|---|---|
| Business scenarios | What business problem and requirements matter? | Problem, environment, actors, objectives, requirements |
| Stakeholder management | Who matters, what concerns them, and how should we engage? | Stakeholder map and communication approach |
| Gap analysis | What exists in Baseline but not Target, or vice versa? | New, retained, and removed elements |
| Migration planning | How should transition states and projects be sequenced? | Roadmap and Implementation & Migration Plan |
| Interoperability analysis | Where must people, process, information, or technology work together? | Interoperability needs and constraints |
| Business Transformation Readiness Assessment | Can the enterprise absorb the proposed change? | Readiness factors, risks, actions |
| Risk management | What uncertainty threatens objectives and how is it treated? | Risks, owners, mitigations, residual exposure |
| Capability-based planning | Which capabilities are needed to realize outcomes? | Capability increments and dependencies |

## Mini-example: gap analysis

Baseline: store inventory updates nightly. Target: near-real-time inventory events. “Nightly batch” is a candidate to remove; “inventory event service” is new; product identifiers may be retained. A gap is not automatically a project: related gaps are later consolidated into solution building blocks and work packages.

## Hands-on: technique chain

For Northstar’s click-and-collect change, list: (1) two stakeholder concerns, (2) three Baseline-to-Target gaps, (3) one readiness risk, and (4) two transition states. Explain the sequence in which you would use the techniques.

## Knowledge check

1. A sponsor asks whether stores can adopt a new operating model. Which technique? 2. Target contains a capability absent from Baseline. What is it? 3. Why is stakeholder analysis not a one-time task?

**Answers:** 1. Business Transformation Readiness Assessment. 2. A gap/addition to address. 3. Stakeholders, concerns, influence, and impacts evolve throughout change.
