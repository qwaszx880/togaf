# F7 — Foundation review and original mock

## Outcomes

Consolidate the Foundation body of knowledge, retrieve core distinctions without prompts, and diagnose errors under timed conditions.

> **Example review:** Revisit the [Foundation examples index](../examples/README.md#foundation-examples) and explain each walkthrough without opening its theory module.

## One-page recall prompts

Without notes: define the four domains; draw the ADM; distinguish E/F and G/H; explain stakeholder/concern/viewpoint/view; list artifact forms; distinguish deliverable/artifact/building block; explain Continuum/Repository.
