# Practitioner example: recover a stalled initiative

[Back to theory: EA context](../../02-practitioner/01-ea-context.md) · [Back to theory: Stakeholder management](../../02-practitioner/02-stakeholders.md)

## Situation

Northstar’s first architecture team published mandatory templates and a target platform. Six months later, regional directors bypass reviews, product teams call architecture slow, and the sponsor cannot name a measurable outcome.

## Facts, assumptions, and first mistake

- **Facts:** low adoption, bypassed reviews, unclear outcome.
- **Assumption to test:** regions resist all shared direction. They may instead resist unclear value or centralized decisions.
- **Mistake:** beginning with content and solution before mandate, outcomes, stakeholders, and capability context.

## Recovery reasoning

1. Interview sponsor and affected leaders to agree purpose and measurable outcomes.
2. Assess capability: authority, skills, processes, repository, delivery integration, and decision latency.
3. Map stakeholders by concern, influence, impact, and attitude.
4. Agree federation: common customer/information contracts centrally governed; compliant regional realization locally governed.
5. Reduce content to decisions and evidence needed for the pilot.
6. measure outcome and governance flow: cancellation, adoption, exception age, decision turnaround.

## Engagement plan excerpt

| Stakeholder | Evidence-based concern | Engagement | Feedback signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regional director | central rules may ignore local systems | co-design decision-rights workshop | accepts or proposes bounded changes |
| Product lead | reviews delay releases | weekly working session and service-level for decisions | decision lead time |
| Sponsor | value is unclear | outcome/roadmap review | funding tied to measurable transition |

## Try it first

Would a stronger Architecture Board mandate solve the problem alone? Write the strongest argument for and against.

**Debrief:** mandate may clarify authority, but cannot substitute for outcomes, suitable federation, useful content, skills, or delivery integration. The best response configures the whole capability.

## Exam insight

When a scenario emphasizes low maturity and resistance, prefer a context-aware, stakeholder-led capability response over either “enforce every standard” or “abandon governance.”
