# Foundation example: two organizations, two valid ADM applications

[Back to theory: Applying the ADM](../../01-foundation/04-applying-adm.md)

## Situation A: regulated bank

A bank changes customer identity verification across twelve products. Legal evidence, risk acceptance, and consistent controls are essential. The bank uses formal approval gates, controlled requirements, detailed security views, and documented compliance reviews. Product teams still deliver incrementally inside those guardrails.

## Situation B: growing retailer

Northstar pilots click-and-collect in nine months. It uses short stakeholder workshops, a lean set of target views, weekly architect/product coordination, and decision checkpoints before pilot and rollout waves.

## Side-by-side tailoring

| Choice | Bank | Northstar | Shared intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | formal risk/legal gates | delegated regional decisions within common contracts | accountable decisions |
| Content depth | detailed control and evidence trace | decision-sufficient domain views | address concerns |
| Iteration | increments within regulatory baseline | learn from store pilots | controlled feedback |
| Partitioning | product ownership with enterprise control model | regional realization with shared semantics | local work stays aligned |

Neither organization is “more TOGAF” because it creates more documents. Both preserve outcomes, necessary controls, traceability, and architecture alignment.

## Try it first

A five-person charity is replacing its donor tool. Select the minimum useful stakeholders, views, decisions, and checkpoints. Write why each retained element matters.

## Worked response

Retain a sponsor decision, donor/privacy concerns, current/target process and information sketch, requirements, options/risks, migration plan, and post-change review. Combine meetings and artifacts where sensible. Omit multiple boards and elaborate domain catalogs because they add little at this scale; record the tailoring rationale.

## Learner variation

The charity begins processing medical-aid information. What changes?

**Debrief:** regulatory/security concerns raise assurance, traceability, information, and governance needs. Organization size alone does not determine rigor; risk and context do.
