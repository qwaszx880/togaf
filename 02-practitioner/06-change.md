# P6 — Architecture Change Management (Phase H)

## Outcomes

Monitor architecture value and environment, assess changes, maintain governance, and select a proportionate response.

> **See it in practice:** Assess and classify a [late regulatory change](../examples/practitioner/05-change-and-requirements.md) without bypassing governance or overreacting.

## Closed-loop change

Monitor business/technology signals and implementation experience → capture request → assess impact against outcomes, requirements, principles, and roadmap → classify response → authorize and update controlled assets → measure results.

Possible responses range from minor controlled update, through partial architecture work, to a new ADM cycle. Classification depends on scope, strategic impact, risk, and whether the vision or target assumptions remain valid—not merely effort in days.

## Useful triggers

- Strategy, regulation, or operating-model change
- New technology opportunity or obsolescence
- Performance or outcome deviation
- Accumulated exceptions and technical debt
- Stakeholder or implementation feedback

## Exercise

Classify three Northstar events and justify: (1) label text correction, (2) new privacy rule affecting reservation data, (3) merger with a company using incompatible inventory semantics. State authority, affected artifacts, and whether a new cycle is warranted.

## Check

**Scenario:** Ten “temporary” standards exceptions accumulate. Best response? **Answer:** assess the pattern and architecture fitness, risks, and drivers through Phase H; update standards/target or initiate architecture work rather than repeatedly waiving symptoms.
