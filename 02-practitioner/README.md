# Certification 2: TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Practitioner

## Goal

Apply and analyze the framework in scenario-based decisions. Practitioner questions reward the response that best fits the evidence, preserves logical ADM sequencing, engages the right stakeholders, uses proportionate governance, and produces useful outcomes.

## The BEST lens

Use **BEST** when choices all sound plausible:

- **B — Business fit:** addresses stated outcomes and concerns.
- **E — Evidence:** responds to facts, constraints, readiness, and risk in the scenario.
- **S — Sequence and scope:** does the right work at the right ADM moment, proportionately.
- **T — Traceability and teamwork:** maintains requirements/decisions and involves accountable stakeholders.

Avoid answers that jump straight to products, prescribe every artifact, bypass authority, or make absolute claims unsupported by the scenario.
