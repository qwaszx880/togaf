# Start here

## What success looks like

Foundation asks, “Do you understand the framework?” Practitioner asks, “Can you select and justify the best TOGAF-aligned response in context?” The second skill depends on the first, but requires judgment rather than extra memorization.

## Learning sequence

| Step | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read [exam facts](../reference/exam-facts.md) | You can explain format, timing, and resources |
| 2 | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) without notes | Baseline score and weak-topic list |
| 3 | Select a [study plan](study-plans.md) | Calendar entries, not merely an intention |
| 4 | Complete Foundation modules in order | Activities and ≥80% module checks |
| 5 | Pass the Foundation mock under timed conditions | ≥80% twice, with explained corrections |
| 6 | Complete the Practitioner path and case artifacts | Decisions tied to scenario evidence |
| 7 | Pass scenario mocks under timed conditions | Consistently identify best and worst choices |

## Prerequisites and materials

- No architecture certification is assumed.
- Obtain lawful access to the applicable official Body of Knowledge. The Practitioner exam’s built-in references are useful only if you know where ideas live.
- Create a notebook with four sections: **terms**, **ADM**, **artifacts**, and **error log**.
- Use a timer for practice; avoid consulting notes until you commit to an answer.

## Study contract

Before beginning, write: “I will study ___ minutes on ___ days. If I miss a session, I will ___. My exam readiness evidence will be ___.” Track work in [progress-tracker.md](progress-tracker.md).
