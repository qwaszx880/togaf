# Further reading and source policy

## Primary sources

1. Start with the [X2202 official syllabus](https://www.opengroup.org/library/x2202); use its learning outcomes as the definitive scope.
2. Obtain the [TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf-standard-10th-edition-downloads) lawfully and observe its license.
3. Confirm operational facts through the [official certification portfolio](https://www.opengroup.org/certifications/togaf) and [examinations site](https://certification.opengroup.org/examinations).
4. Use official TOGAF Series Guides included in the applicable Body of Knowledge to deepen applied topics.

## How to reconcile this course and official material

This course supplies intuition, deliberate practice, and a continuous case. Official sources control terminology, learning outcomes, and exam policy. When wording differs, record the official definition in your error log, link it to the plain-language explanation here, and practice both recognition and application.

## Responsible study

Avoid “exam dumps,” reconstructed live questions, and unauthorized copies. Besides ethical and contractual concerns, they train brittle recognition. Prefer syllabus-mapped retrieval, artifact creation, scenario ranking, and explaining why alternatives are weaker.
