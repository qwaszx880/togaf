# Exam facts and official sources

> Verify details when booking: providers can update policies, languages, delivery, and fees. Last verified **2026-10-03** from the official pages below.

## Current path

The Open Group calls these qualifications **TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation** and **TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Practitioner**. Candidates may take Part 1 then Part 2, or a combined Part 1 and Part 2 route. TOGAF 9 Certified candidates also have a Bridge path; prerequisites and registration status should be checked officially.

| Certification / exam | Code | Format | Time | Pass mark | Book policy |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Foundation / Part 1 | OGEA-101 | 40 simple multiple-choice items | 60 min | 60% | Closed book |
| Practitioner / Part 2 | OGEA-102 | 8 scenario-based, gradient-scored items | 90 min | 60% | Open book: built-in reference |
| Combined | OGEA-103 | Part 1 plus Part 2 sections | Confirm at booking | Each section must meet its requirement | Part 1 closed; Part 2 built-in reference |

In Part 2, the best answer earns 5 points, the next 3, the next 1, and the worst 0. “Open book” does not mean arbitrary websites or personal notes; use the electronic reference supplied through the exam interface. The official help article describes two supplied PDF groupings: Fundamental Content and applicable TOGAF Series Guides.

## Practitioner major areas

EA context; Stakeholder Management; Phase A; Architecture Development (B–D); Implementing the Architecture (E–G); Architecture Change Management (H); Requirements Management; and Supporting ADM Work. A scenario can span several areas.

## Exam-day checklist

- Confirm ID, appointment time/time zone, test-center or online-proctor rules, system test, language/ESL allowance, and reschedule policy with the provider.
- Part 1: target about 75 seconds per item; answer all, mark uncertainty, revisit.
- Part 2: read the ask and facts, rank all choices, use reference search only to resolve targeted uncertainty.
- Never use recalled or shared live questions; practice transferable reasoning.
